# 📌 About the Internship

This repository contains the documentation and work related to my industrial internship at **Intelligentia Labs Pvt. Ltd.**, working with the **InventoJoy** consumer toy brand.

The internship focused on **graphics design, packaging design, product prototyping, instructional design, design-for-manufacturing (DFM), and marketing creatives**.

The role provided practical exposure to the complete design-to-production workflow, where visual design had to be considered alongside structural, manufacturing, logistics, regulatory, and usability constraints.

The internship was a full-time, work-from-office engagement based in EPIP Site-5, Greater Noida. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

# 🎯 Objectives of the Internship

The major objectives of the internship were:

1. Apply graphic design fundamentals to toy packaging and printed collateral such as boxes, dielines, inserts, and stickers.
2. Develop practical knowledge of structural packaging and dieline design.
3. Assist in building physical prototypes and testing products for functional and durability requirements.
4. Design intuitive and child-friendly instruction manuals and assembly guides.
5. Collaborate with product and manufacturing teams to develop Design-for-Manufacturing-ready solutions.
6. Support marketing teams with digital and print creatives.
7. Develop professional skills such as version control, documentation, vendor handover, feedback handling, and working with production deadlines. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
